Protonation at a glance

Drag the pH. Below a group's pKa it holds its proton; above it, the proton is gone. This one idea drives most of the acid/base and amino acid questions on the exam.

Shaded left of the pKa means protonated; right means deprotonated. The percentages come straight from Henderson–Hasselbalch, so the bar at pH = pKa always reads 50/50. Side-chain pKa values shift with the local environment inside a folded protein — histidine is the classic case.
